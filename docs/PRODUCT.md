# ApkaLawyer — Product Requirement & Vision Document

## 1. Executive Summary & Vision
ApkaLawyer is Pakistan's dedicated digital legal marketplace and case management platform. It transforms how Pakistani citizens, overseas Pakistanis, startups, and enterprises discover, evaluate, consult, and collaborate with verified legal advocates across all jurisdictions (District Courts, High Courts, and Supreme Court).

## 2. Target User Personas

### 2.1 Clients
- **Individual Citizens**: Family disputes, property verification, succession certificates, consumer protection, bail matters, inheritance advice.
- **Overseas Pakistanis**: Power of Attorney execution, overseas property dispute representation, remote notarization, remote court representation.
- **SMEs & Startups**: Company incorporation (SECP), intellectual property (IPO Pakistan), employment agreements, NDAs, contract dispute resolution.
- **Corporate Legal Departments**: Outside counsel engagement, retainer tracking, cause list monitoring, hearing calendar management.

### 2.2 Legal Practitioners (Lawyers / Advocates)
- **Advocate High Court / Supreme Court**: Client acquisition, digital fee collection, court diary digitisation, client document repository.
- **Young Advocates / Law Firms**: Expanding digital reach across cities (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Peshawar, Quetta, Multan).
- **Specialized Counsel**: Tax advocates (FBR), Banking court litigators, NAB/FIA defense specialists, Family law experts.

### 2.3 System Administrators & Regulators
- Bar Council verification review (Punjab Bar, Sindh Bar, Islamabad Bar, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Bar, Balochistan Bar).
- Trust & safety oversight, fee dispute escrow resolution, quality auditing.

---

## 3. Core Product Modules

### 3.1 Lawyer Directory & Smart Matching
- Multi-faceted filters: Practice area, city/court level, language (Urdu, English, Punjabi, Pashto, Sindhi, Balochi), years of experience, consultation fee, rating.
- Verified badge displaying verified Bar License Number & enrollment date.
- Transparent fee cards: 15-min discovery call, 45-min detailed consultation, written legal opinion, court appearance retainer.

### 3.2 Booking & Consultation Engine
- Real-time time slot selector respecting advocate availability.
- Multi-mode consultations: Audio/Video consultation, In-person office appointment, Written legal advice.
- Instant intake questionnaire attaching preliminary documents prior to consultation.

### 3.3 Legal Matters (Case Management)
- Matter overview with progress timeline, milestones, assigned counsel, and financial ledger.
- Collaborative timeline showing petition drafting, court filing, notices served, stay orders, final arguments, judgment decree.

### 3.4 Cause List & Hearing Tracker
- Smart court diary tracking upcoming hearing dates, court number, presiding judge, and hearing purpose (e.g. Framed Issues, Evidence, Final Arguments).
- Automated SMS/WhatsApp notifications prior to cause list appearances.

### 3.5 Secure Document Vault
- Encrypted repository for petitions, stay orders, power of attorney (Vakalatnama), title deeds, CNIC copies, and certified court transcripts.
- Access-control matrix allowing time-bound sharing between client and lawyer.

### 3.6 Automated Legal Document Generator
- Wizard-driven draft generator for common Pakistani legal formats:
  - Legal Notice (Defamation / Section 30 / Recovery)
  - Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA - Pakistan Jurisdiction)
  - Tenancy / Rental Agreement (Islamabad/Punjab/Sindh format)
  - Employment & Independent Contractor Agreements
  - Power of Attorney (General & Special)
- Clear disclaimer: *Drafts require independent lawyer review before official execution.*

### 3.7 Financials & Retainer Management
- Itemized invoicing: Consultation fees, court filing expenses, retainer tranches.
- Transparent payment status (Pending, In Escrow, Released, Refunded).

### 3.8 Reviews & Quality System
- Verified client reviews post-consultation with granular ratings (Legal Knowledge, Responsiveness, Transparency).
